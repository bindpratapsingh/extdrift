extdrift

Behavioural differencing of browser-extension updates. Score the change, not the extension.

1 Pick two versions of one extension

Version N is the release already installed and trusted. Version N+1 is the update about to replace it. Pick a ready-made pair below — one click fills both — or choose each package yourself.
